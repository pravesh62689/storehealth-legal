This Privacy Policy explains what data the StoreHealth app ("StoreHealth," "the app," "we," "us") accesses when installed on a Shopify store, why it accesses that data, how it is stored, and how it is deleted. StoreHealth is developed and operated by Apex Forge Technologies, a sole proprietorship based in India.

If you have questions about this policy or want to make a data request, contact: apexforgetechnologies@gmail.com

1. What StoreHealth reads

StoreHealth is a read-only app. It never creates, edits, or deletes data in your store. To run its daily store health audit, it reads:

  • Product data: title, status (active/draft/archived), publication state, images, price, compare-at price
  • Variant data: inventory tracking settings, weight, shipping requirements
  • Inventory data: quantity on hand and availability, by location
  • Location data: store locations associated with inventory
  • Order data — exactly five fields only:
    • id
    • name
    • createdAt
    • displayFulfillmentStatus
    • cancelledAt

StoreHealth uses this data to detect: products that are out of stock but still published to your storefront, products missing images, variants missing shipping weight where shipping is required, orders that have remained unfulfilled past a merchant-configurable threshold (default 7 days), and products where the compare-at price is lower than the price (a pricing error).

2. What StoreHealth does NOT read

StoreHealth's order queries request no customer object whatsoever. Specifically, StoreHealth does not access, process, transmit, or store:

  • Customer names
  • Customer email addresses
  • Customer phone numbers
  • Customer shipping or billing addresses
  • Any other customer personal data

No GraphQL query issued by StoreHealth contains a customer field or any customer-identifying data. This is a deliberate design choice, not an incidental limitation: StoreHealth's audit logic operates entirely on product, inventory, and order-status data and has no need for customer personal data to function.

3. Why we process this data

The sole purpose of the data StoreHealth reads is to power its daily automated store health audit and present the results on your StoreHealth dashboard. We do not use this data for any other purpose. We do not use it for marketing, advertising, profiling, or any form of automated decision-making that affects you or your customers. We do not sell this data, and we do not share it with third parties except the infrastructure sub-processors described in Section 5.

4. Where data is stored and how it is protected

  • Database: Neon (managed PostgreSQL), encrypted at rest
  • Transit: All data in transit between Shopify, StoreHealth's servers, and its database is encrypted using TLS
  • What is actually stored: For flagged orders specifically, StoreHealth stores only the order id and name — never the other three fields it reads for computation, and never any customer data. Product and inventory data used for the audit is processed and reflected in dashboard results but is not retained beyond what is needed to compute and display the current and recent audit state.

5. Retention

StoreHealth retains the results of the last 30 audit runs per store. Older audit run data is pruned automatically. We do not keep an indefinite history of your store's data.

6. Deletion

When you uninstall StoreHealth, Shopify sends an app/uninstalled webhook to our servers. On receipt of that webhook, all data associated with your shop is hard-deleted from our database. This is an automated process; no manual request is required to have your shop's data removed upon uninstall.

You may also request deletion of your data at any time before uninstalling by contacting us at the email address above.

7. Sub-processors

StoreHealth relies on the following infrastructure providers to operate:

  • Neon — managed PostgreSQL database hosting
  • Fly.io — application hosting

These providers process data solely as infrastructure on our behalf, under their own security and data protection commitments. We do not use any sub-processor for marketing, analytics resale, or any purpose unrelated to operating StoreHealth.

8. No sale, no sharing, no profiling

We do not sell any data accessed by StoreHealth. We do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. We do not build customer profiles. We do not use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on any person. All of StoreHealth's checks are deterministic, rule-based comparisons (e.g., "is quantity zero and status published") — there is no machine learning model, scoring, or profiling involved.

9. Your rights

Because StoreHealth does not process customer personal data at all, there is no customer data for a shopper to request access to, correct, or delete from StoreHealth specifically. If you are a merchant using StoreHealth and want to review, export, or delete the limited order-identifier data we retain (order ID and name for flagged orders, per Section 4), contact us at the email above and we will act on your request within a reasonable time.

10. Changes to this policy

If we materially change what data StoreHealth accesses or how it is used, we will update this policy and change the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of StoreHealth after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
