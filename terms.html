These terms describe how StoreHealth ("the app") handles your store's data. By installing StoreHealth, you agree to these terms.

StoreHealth is developed and operated by Apex Forge Technologies, a sole proprietor developer based in India, referred to here as "we," "us," or "the developer."

1. What StoreHealth does

StoreHealth runs a daily automated audit of your store and reports operational issues it finds: products that are out of stock but still published, products missing images, variants missing shipping weight, orders left unfulfilled past a threshold you can configure, and products with pricing errors (compare-at price lower than price).

StoreHealth is read-only. It does not create, modify, or delete any product, inventory, order, or customer record in your store. It only reads data to compute and display audit results.

2. Data we access

We access product, variant, inventory, and location data, and exactly five order fields: id, name, createdAt, displayFulfillmentStatus, and cancelledAt. We do not request, read, or store any customer personal data — no names, emails, phone numbers, or addresses. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

3. Controller and processor roles

For the purposes of applicable data protection law, you (the merchant) are the data controller of your store's data, including any personal data associated with your orders and customers. We act as a data processor, processing the limited data described above solely to provide the StoreHealth service to you, and solely on your instructions as expressed through your use of the app.

We do not use your store's data for any purpose beyond providing StoreHealth's audit functionality to you.

4. Sub-processors

We use the following sub-processors to operate StoreHealth:

  • Neon (managed PostgreSQL database)
  • Fly.io (application hosting)

We remain responsible for these sub-processors' handling of your data in connection with StoreHealth. If we add or change a sub-processor in a way that materially affects how your data is handled, we will update our Privacy Policy and note the change date.

5. Security measures

  • Data is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • Data is encrypted at rest in our managed database provider.
  • Access to production data is limited to the developer; StoreHealth is currently operated by a single individual, not a team, and no third-party staff have data access.
  • We do not maintain a separate marketing or analytics copy of your data outside the systems described here.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain the results of your last 30 audit runs. For orders flagged by the audit, we retain only the order ID and name — never other order or customer fields. Older audit history beyond the most recent 30 runs is automatically pruned.

On uninstall, Shopify notifies us via the app/uninstalled webhook, and we automatically and permanently delete all data associated with your shop. This happens without further action from you.

You can request earlier deletion, or ask what data we hold about your shop, at any time by contacting support@apexforgetech.com.

7. No sale, no profiling, no automated decisions

We do not sell your data. We do not use it to build profiles of you or your customers. We do not use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects. Every check StoreHealth runs is a deterministic rule (for example: "is this product published with zero inventory"), not a model or algorithm that makes judgments about people.

8. Breach notification

If we become aware of a security incident that compromises your shop's data while in our custody, we will notify you at the store contact email on file without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours of confirming the incident, describing what happened, what data was affected, and what we are doing about it.

We will reasonably assist you in meeting those obligations where they concern data StoreHealth processes, but we do not provide legal advice and this agreement is not a substitute for your own legal compliance review.
